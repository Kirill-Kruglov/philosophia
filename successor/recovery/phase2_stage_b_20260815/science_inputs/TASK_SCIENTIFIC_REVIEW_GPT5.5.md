# PHILOSOPHIA_MINIMUM_CAUSAL_CONTRACT_V1

## 0. Authority and scope

This is a prospective scientific contract for the minimum bounded Phase-2 route. It authorizes no code, no experiment, no fixtures, no roots, no commits, no pushes, and no statistical implementation.

Fixed route decisions are not reopened:

- original B2 path manufacture is `VOID_BY_CONSTRUCTION`;
- audit-14b remains `POLICY_CHANNEL_VIABLE` only, not SELF/YOKED evidence;
- the primary route is a thin MINIMO reciprocal slice;
- Stage-B universality is not on the scientific critical path;
- truthful-history and transfer are registered as conditional Stage H, run only after Stage R earns its positive terminal.

The unrestricted existential programme claim cannot be finitely falsified. This contract therefore defines bounded claims over one frozen world family, learner class, compute envelope and endpoint.

## 1. Claim stack E/R/H

E — unrestricted programme claim: There exists some world and learner for which self-selected contact creates transferable experience.

R — bounded reciprocal-selection claim: In the frozen MINIMO Stage-B erased-proof-plan theorem family with L3 alpha-canonical public identity, sealed public projection, minimum semantic L4 compile/replay, a small sealed scientific reservoir and held-out panel, for the repaired-from-scratch Stage-A-compatible MINIMO learner class under the signed Phase-2 compute envelope `AUTHOR_CHOICE_BEFORE_IMPLEMENTATION`, recipient-state-matched task selection reduces held-out capped entered MCTS work relative to reciprocal matched donated selection by the prospectively registered Stage-R endpoint.

H — bounded truthful-history-and-transfer claim: Conditional on Stage R positive, in the same frozen MINIMO family plus preregistered held-out theorem families and one semantics-preserving representation change, for branches inheriting identical weights and equal history access budget `AUTHOR_CHOICE_BEFORE_IMPLEMENTATION`, truthful verified retained history reduces work-to-threshold beyond weights-only and beyond size-, shape- and interface-matched false/permuted/content-destroyed history.

## 2. Earned evidence and occupied claims

Earned local evidence is limited. MINIMO Phase 1 showed one CPU-debug artifact property: checkpoint 1 lowered capped held-out entered MCTS iterations versus checkpoint 0, with no population, stability, SELF/YOKED, or Philosophia claim. Stage A and Stage-B L0–L2 are feasibility/instrument artifacts only. Audit-14b qualifies a development policy channel but is not a self/yoked carrier, reciprocal gate, history test, or transfer result.

Occupied claims include generic self-generated verified learning, active-versus-random selection, learned-weight transfer, useful relevant memory, process supervision, and work reduction from learned prover weights. The residual novelty is only the conjunction of:

1. recipient-state-specific task value under reciprocal matched yoke;
2. truthful retained-history value beyond weights and ledger shape;
3. work reduction on held-out families and under semantics-preserving representation change.

Stage A/B feasibility artifacts provide no scientific evidence for R or H.

## 3. Stage R estimand and design

Stage R uses exact independently initialized learner twins. For recipient state `r` and task-batch source `q`, each valid block contains all four branches:

- `A←A`
- `A←B`
- `B←A`
- `B←B`

The primary block contrast is:

`D_j = mean_g[(X_A←B - X_A←A + X_B←A - X_B←B) / 2]`

with prospectively registered stratum weights. `X` is the held-out work endpoint, lower is better. Positive `D_j` favors recipient-state-matched selection.

One independently initialized twin pair with all four valid branches is one independent unit. Theorems are repeated measurements within the unit and cannot inflate `N`.

R positive alone does not establish the main Philosophia claim. It establishes only bounded recipient-state-specific task value in this cell.

## 4. Stage R validity controls

Required controls:

- exact same-state/same-batch replay: same serialized state plus same batch must produce byte-identical examples, optimizer state, weights and evaluation;
- `COLD-SELF` zero-divergence: exact cold twins must select identically and yield zero reciprocal interaction;
- non-degenerate acquired-state divergence before the reciprocal run;
- selector sign and scale parity;
- selector incremental predictive value beyond statement-only surface features;
- synthetic injected-coupling recovery before any null can be interpreted;
- whole-block validity, whole-block retry rules, attrition ledger, no replacement seeds, and worst-case missing-`D_j` bounds.

Failure of any control produces `R_INVALID`, never a negative scientific result.

## 5. Stage H conditional design

Stage H is registered now but not implemented or run unless Stage R reaches its prospectively defined positive terminal.

Conditional arms must inherit identical weights and receive equal ledger byte/token/access budgets:

- weights only;
- weights plus truthful verified history;
- weights plus false, permuted or content-destroyed history with identical public schema and interface.

Evaluation must include held-out theorem families, a semantics-preserving representation change, and work-to-threshold as the primary endpoint. A ledger-form benefit reproduced by false history is not truth-specific experience.

Stage H controls:

- identical inherited checkpoint;
- equal ledger byte/token/access budget;
- truthful and false records with identical public schema;
- no theorem, plan or root identity leakage;
- held-out family separation;
- semantics-preserving re-encoding;
- weights-only and no-history baselines;
- deterministic replay.

## 6. Endpoints and independent units

Primary assigned-work measure: capped entered MCTS iterations, unless an accepted driver explicitly supersedes it before implementation.

Mandatory companion endpoints:

- solve rate;
- successful new-leaf expansions;
- LM-query work;
- realized example volume.

Stage H primary endpoint: work-to-threshold on held-out families and representation-shift evaluation. Threshold, margin, block count, stratum weights and compute envelope remain `AUTHOR_CHOICE_BEFORE_IMPLEMENTATION`.

The learner-twin block is the independent unit. Theorem-level observations are repeated measurements only.

## 10. Hard stops

- One authoring pass and one independent contract review.
- At most one bounded repair pass.
- No fourth general review.
- Minimal L4 compile/replay must close within at most three focused implementation days after its accepted paper boundary exists.
- Failure of the L4 kill closes the MINIMO route and returns to `IDEA_GATE`.
- Selector qualification failure closes the selector route.
- Positive-control or deterministic-replay failure makes the experiment invalid.
- No automatic switch to a larger model, longer budget, richer world or new proof-DAG.

Inferential hierarchy:

- R positive alone does not establish the main Philosophia claim.
- R null/negative closes the own-state-selection branch in this cell and H is not run.
- R positive followed by H failure yields a boundary result.
- Only R positive plus H truth-specific transfer supports the bounded strong claim.
- No result universally proves or falsifies E.
