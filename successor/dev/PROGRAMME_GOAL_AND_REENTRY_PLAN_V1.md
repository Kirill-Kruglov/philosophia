# Philosophia — programme goal and scientific re-entry plan V1

Status: `PROPOSED_FOR_AUTHOR_DISCUSSION`
Date: 2026-08-16

This is a planning object, not a result, preregistration or authorization. It
does not reopen Level 1, MINIMO Stage R, E2, the Wall-B policy carrier or any
scientific execution.

## 1. The unchanged programme question

Can a small learner trained from scratch obtain primary experience from a
derivable world, where experience is not mere task success but retained
structure that is causally traceable, reduces future work and survives a change
of world or representation?

The current programme claim is `OPEN`. No ACTIVE/YOKED comparison or successor
scientific execution has occurred.

## 2. Verification and falsification are asymmetric

The broad claim is existential. One correctly isolated witness can establish a
bounded existence result. A sequence of failed learners, sparse carriers or
unbuildable instruments cannot falsify existence in general.

Therefore:

- **bounded verification** requires one functioning carrier and a valid causal
  witness for at least `PROOF_CORE` (`C2 ∧ C3 ∧ C4`);
- **class-bounded negative evidence** may kill a prospectively named learner,
  carrier or interface class, but not the global existential claim;
- **programme-level falsification** requires either a prospectively narrowed
  universal claim or a theoretical impossibility/non-identifiability result for
  the stated class. TWOPRES is an example of the latter shape, not a global
  falsification.

This asymmetry is the central goal-setting constraint. More failed feasibility
screens do not converge automatically toward falsification.

## 3. Immediate objective: publication closure

The immediate task is to finish an honest publication unit at the current
evidential altitude:

1. run two independent, non-overlapping reviews of the reconciled essay;
2. make one author disposition;
3. permit at most one local repair pass for findings caused by the reconciled
   delta;
4. freeze the essay or stop publication preparation with the exact unresolved
   defect.

This review is not a gate to new experiments and cannot authorize a substrate.

## 4. Recommended scientific objective after publication

Prioritize a **bounded `PROOF_CORE` witness**, not `PROOF_STRONG`.

`PROOF_CORE` asks whether retained truthful history:

- `C2`: reduces work on held-out future problems;
- `C3`: survives a semantics-preserving change of representation;
- `C4`: contributes causally beyond weights alone and beyond a false ledger.

`PROOF_STRONG` additionally requires chosen-contact superiority (`C1`) and
non-redundant path credit (`C5`). Those are more expensive and should not be the
entry ticket for learning whether any manufactured experience exists at all.

## 5. Scientific re-entry gate

No active carrier search or new carrier construction is recommended. Re-entry
is event-driven: consider a new line only when an independently motivated,
already functioning external system satisfies all of the following before
Philosophia-specific construction begins.

1. **Carrier exists:** tasks, verifier, learner/update and held-out evaluation
   already run end to end.
2. **Native competence exists:** the learner clears a prospectively declared
   competence floor without a Philosophia treatment.
3. **Truth is independent:** labels and held-out scoring do not come from the
   learner or selector under test.
4. **Causal isolation is affordable:** weights, truthful history and false or
   absent history can be separated without building a new research platform.
5. **Transfer is real:** the carrier supplies a held-out family or
   semantics-preserving interface change, not only more items from one encoding.
6. **Work is countable:** a host-independent assigned-work counter exists before
   outcomes; wall time is only operational metadata.
7. **Controls exist:** an executable zero control and injected positive control
   can show that the instrument detects both absence and presence of the target
   effect.
8. **Cheap first number exists:** one decisive disposable observation fits the
   standing eight-focused-hour pre-harness ceiling. If the probe is already the
   minimum experiment, re-entry fails as it did for E2.

Passing this gate authorizes only a new author decision and standalone charter.
It does not inherit authority from the stopped MINIMO route.

## 7. Event-driven carrier watch

A candidate may trigger one bounded desk screen only if it arrives for an
independent reason: a released system, a functioning internal project, or a
published benchmark already containing most of the eight re-entry properties.

The screen asks only:

```text
CARRIER_ALREADY_FUNCTIONS=YES|NO
NATIVE_COMPETENCE_ALREADY_SHOWN=YES|NO
INDEPENDENT_TRUTH_AND_TRANSFER=YES|NO
CAUSAL_DECOMPOSITION_WITHOUT_NEW_PLATFORM=YES|NO
HOST_INDEPENDENT_WORK_COUNTER_ALREADY_EXISTS=YES|NO
ZERO_AND_INJECTED_CONTROLS_ALREADY_EXECUTABLE=YES|NO
DECISIVE_PROBE_WITHIN_8_HOURS=YES|NO
```

Any `NO` ends the screen. No repair pass follows. A full `YES` returns to the
author for a new-line decision; it does not start implementation automatically.

## 8. Review-loop stop rule

For the present essay review:

- the formal and structural reviews run once, in parallel, from identical
  pinned bytes and do not read each other;
- only a concrete false claim, unsupported inference, broken citation boundary
  or structural contradiction can require revision;
- disagreement about taste is an author decision, not another reviewer round;
- one bounded repair may address the union of accepted findings;
- the repair receives one mechanical diff audit, not two fresh full reviews;
- any new Critical discovered outside the changed regions is recorded for a
  future edition unless it proves the current publication materially false.

This makes review an instrument for publication correctness rather than a new
research programme.

## 10. What progress means now

Progress is not the number of protocols written. It is one of:

1. a publication whose claims exactly match the evidence;
2. a functioning carrier that passes the re-entry gate without being built for
   the claim;
3. a bounded causal witness for `C2 ∧ C3 ∧ C4`;
4. a proved impossibility or non-identifiability boundary for an explicitly
   stated class.

Until one of 2–4 exists, the scientifically correct state remains `OPEN`.
