## 2. Why there are two reviews

The reviewers have different attack surfaces and run in parallel from the same
bytes. Neither reads or responds to the other reviewer. They are not successive
rounds and do not repair each other's findings.

### Formal/evidential line

Checks factual entailment, mathematical scope, statistical wording, citation
support and status-class preservation. It asks whether each sentence says no
more than its evidence.

### Philosophical/structural line

Checks argumentative architecture, voice, cadence, antecedents, negative space
and whether the enlarged ledger still serves the essay rather than replacing
its conclusion.

## 7. Output standard

Each reviewer writes exactly one response file. Findings are ordered
Critical/Major/Minor and cite exact current file:line locations.

Every Critical or Major must include:

1. the exact sentence or structural unit;
2. the evidence or argument showing the defect;
3. the smallest bounded repair;
4. whether the finding is caused by the reconciliation delta.

A preference about cadence, terminology or placement is Minor unless it causes
ambiguity about what was observed, claimed or concluded.

do not receive each other's output.

- A pre-existing materially false claim: repair only that claim in the same
  union pass.
- Other pre-existing findings and all optional Minors: record for a future
  edition; they do not keep this review open.
- The union repair receives one diff audit and no fresh full review.
- A new disagreement after the union repair is an author disposition, not a
  third reviewer round.
