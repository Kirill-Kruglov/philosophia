## What “cheapest observation answering both” actually is

The gate demands one observation that jointly answers:

1. **Acquisition / non-surface selection:** acquired twins yield nondegenerate batches that are not equivalent under a statement-only surface scorer, with no witness or held-out leakage into the selector.
2. **Estimator integrity:** reciprocal `D_j` recovers a planted interaction and is exactly zero under identical state.

**Q2 alone** (synthetic `X` matrices, planted coupling, identical-state clone) is a few-hour algebra/harness smoke test. It does **not** touch acquisition, public-only divergence, or surface-masquerade. Counting it as the E2 disposable measurement would smuggle a registered design past IDEA_GATE §5–6.

**Q1 alone** already requires: sealed public reservoir of compositional tasks, verifier, two acquirable states, public-only selector, surface-feature baseline, and a divergence protocol without witnesses. That is the scientific selector instrument, not a pre-harness probe.

**Both together** = Q1 stack + branch isolation + held-out counter + zero control + injected coupling. That is the minimum reciprocal experiment under another name. Launch packet §4 forbids growing a general generator/compiler/root framework around it; even without that growth, the joint object is the programme.

There is no intermediate “cheap decisive” that kills the carrier/selector **before** that stack: the failure modes that matter (surface selection; oracle/witness leakage; non-recoverable interaction) are properties of the stack itself.
