Answers:

1. Is the exact empirical interaction already measured?

No located primary work measures exactly:

`utility(verified batch selected by recipient state) - utility(verified batch selected by other twin state)`

under exact learner twins, one common reservoir, identity-disjoint held-out work, and the complete reciprocal 2x2 estimator. The closest prior art is yoked learning, especially Markant–Gureckis and YoL, but those designs do not cancel additive recipient competence and additive batch quality with the four-arm reciprocal block.

2. If not, what kind of contribution is measuring it?

It is a meaningful causal-method contribution, not broad novelty. It would test whether “own-state selection” has recipient-specific task value after stronger controls than active-vs-random or one-way yoked comparisons. It is also a useful negative calibration for the essay: a clean null or opposite-sign result would say that this proposed Philosophia residue does not survive even in a purpose-built small verified cell.

It is not enough to claim novelty from conjunction alone. The work is worth doing only if the cell is non-tautological and the selector is not given hidden missing-skill labels, proof witnesses, held-out dependency information, or construction-forced relevance.

3. Would a purpose-built toy cell add information beyond the 2026 theory?

Yes, conditionally. Learning to Reason with Curriculum I/II already establishes that autocurriculum and compositional decomposition can be theoretically powerful. A toy E2 cell adds information only if it measures a finite realized interaction that the theory leaves open: whether the selected batch’s utility is recipient-state-specific under reciprocal exchange.

It would merely instantiate a construction-forced theorem if the cell encodes “the learner selects exactly the missing reusable relation” or otherwise makes own-state benefit analytic. The launch-packet requirement that the treatment can be zero or either sign is therefore essential. Without that, the experiment fails IDEA_GATE as tautological.

4. Narrowest publishable wording

Positive wording:

“In one frozen verified composition cell and learner class, with exact learner twins and a common task reservoir, batches selected from the recipient’s own state reduced capped verifier/search work on identity-disjoint held-out tasks more than reciprocally donated batches, under a preregistered complete 2x2 yoke. This supports a bounded recipient-state-specific task-value interaction; it does not establish general self-improvement, open-ended discovery, cross-world transfer, or retained-history value.”

Negative wording:

“In one frozen verified composition cell and learner class, the reciprocal 2x2 yoke did not show a preregistered practical own-state-selection advantage on identity-disjoint held-out work. This bounds the proposed mechanism in this cell; it does not falsify the unrestricted existential programme claim.”
