## 2. Current novelty correction

Broad claims are occupied: self-generated verified improvement, learned-weight
work reduction, learner-conditioned curricula and generic compositional
curriculum benefit are not Philosophia novelty.

Two especially close 2026 results sharpen the boundary:

- Rajaraman et al., *Learning to Reason with Curriculum I*, prove benefits from
  choosing training prompts using current-model performance:
  <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/rajaraman26a.html>.
- Rajaraman et al., *Learning to Reason with Curriculum II: Compositional
  Generalization*, prove large statistical gains from recursively learning and
  composing shorter semiautomaton computations:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27721>.

STP already combines conjecturing and proving at the learner's frontier
(<https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00212>), and MM-ACL predicts improvement from a
training task using current student status
(<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v232/xu23a.html>). Consequently a new E2 cell is
scientifically defensible only as a causal identification experiment for the
remaining recipient-specific interaction, not as evidence that curriculum,
composition, proof generation or verified self-training works in general.

## 3. The only candidate admitted to this gate

`E2` is a prospective, purpose-built, small verified composition cell. It is not
yet a design. To pass the paper gate, a candidate instantiation must expose all
of the following without relying on MINIMO/Peano:

1. a finite public task language and a deterministic independent verifier;
2. proofs or solution traces requiring composition of at least two reusable
   relations, not a count, lookup key, answer token or hidden label;
3. a learner state, a common public reservoir, a state-conditioned selector and
   one aggregate update;
4. exact twin states and the complete reciprocal branches `A<-A`, `A<-B`,
   `B<-A`, `B<-B`;
5. an identity-disjoint held-out panel on which capped future work is measured;
6. no selector access to proof witnesses, hidden skill labels, held-out
   dependencies or an oracle statement of what each learner lacks;
7. a treatment contrast that can be zero or have either sign under admissible
   parameterizations. If a positive contrast is forced by construction, the
   cell is tautological and fails.

The bounded candidate claim is:

> In one frozen E2 cell and learner class, a batch selected from the recipient's
> own state reduces identity-disjoint held-out verifier/search work more than the
> reciprocal batch selected from the other twin's state, by a prospectively
> registered practical margin.

Composition qualifies the carrier. The reciprocal interaction is the only
candidate scientific contribution. Truthful-history/Stage H remains outside
this line unless a Stage-R cell first produces a positive result.

## 6. Pass rule and finite topology

E2 advances only if all three independently hold:

1. **Identifiable and non-tautological:** Task A supplies a complete minimal
   construction and two explicit admissible parameterizations with different
   signs or zero/nonzero contrast, with no forbidden selector information.
2. **Right-sized scientific residue:** Task B finds that the exact reciprocal
   recipient-specific interaction is not already answered and is worth an
   empirical estimate even if the result is a bounded null.
3. **Cheap first number:** Task C identifies a disposable measurement that can
   kill the carrier or selector before a scientific harness is built, with no
   more than eight focused engineering hours and no scientific outcomes.

Failure of any one condition returns `STOP_STAGE_R_E2_BEFORE_BUILD`. Passing all
three authorizes only an author decision on a one-page disposable-probe
contract. It does not authorize implementation or execution.

Review topology:

```text
three parallel evidence tasks

        -> either STOP or one disposable-probe paper contract
```

There is no general review, repair, rereview sequence at IDEA_GATE.
